Privacy Policy

Prizebond-Tracker Pakistan · by AnchorSofts · Last updated: 14 October 2026

Prizebond-Tracker Pakistan ("the app") helps you check Pakistani prize bond numbers against published draw results. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: we do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data.

Information stored on your device

  • Your saved bonds (bond numbers, denominations and any labels you add) and your settings (language, tax filer status) are stored only on your phone.
  • This data never leaves your device and is not sent to AnchorSofts or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Camera

  • The camera is used only when you choose to scan bond numbers.
  • Text recognition runs entirely on your device. Photos are not uploaded, saved by the app, or shared.

Internet use

  • The app may download public draw results and the draw schedule to keep its data up to date. These downloads do not include any information about you or your bonds.
  • Links to anchorsofts.com or other websites open in your browser and are governed by those websites' own policies.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in, no analytics or tracking, no advertising, and no location access.
  • No personal data is sold or shared with third parties.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Disclaimer

This is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Central Directorate of National Savings, the State Bank of Pakistan, or the Government of Pakistan. Always confirm a win with official results before claiming a prize. The "Lucky Lever" produces random numbers for fun only and does not predict draw results.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
